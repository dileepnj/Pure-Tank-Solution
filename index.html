Why clean your water tank?

Over time, mud, algae and bacteria build up inside water tanks. Dirty tanks can make your household water unsafe, even when it looks clear.

Protect your health

Removes germs and algae that can cause stomach illness and skin problems.

Better tasting water

No more muddy smell or odd taste from a neglected tank.

Longer tank life

Regular cleaning stops sludge build-up and keeps pipes and taps flowing.
